Renderer: `Visual.setNotice(text | null)` shows a notice between the header and the cards, styled from a new `notice` token group (also covered by `highContrastTheme`); `Visual.setHeader({ eyebrow, title, show, showSubtitle })` edits the heading text and hides the heading or the subtitle after creation. `DEFAULT_HEADING` exports the default eyebrow and title.
