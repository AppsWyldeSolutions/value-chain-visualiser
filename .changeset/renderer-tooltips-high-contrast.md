Renderer: `createVisual` takes a `tooltip` option (`show`, `move`, `hide`), called as the pointer moves over KPIs and cards, in place of the native title tooltips; `tooltipItems(target)` gives a target's lines. `highContrastTheme(colors)` and `Visual.setHighContrast(colors | null)` draw the visual flat in a high-contrast mode's colours: no gradients, glass, glow or highlights, solid borders, missed spheres in the hyperlink colour.
